You are reading a document sent to the accounts payable team of Meridian Supplies (the buyer).
You are given images of every page and the OCR text of the same pages.

Return ONE JSON object and nothing else (no explanations, no code fences), with exactly these keys:

{
  "doc_class": "invoice | credit_note | multi_invoice | delivery_note | other",
  "vendor_name": "string or null",
  "invoice_number": "string or null",
  "invoice_date": "YYYY-MM-DD or null",

- currency: the ISO code, e.g. INR for "Rs.", "₹" or "INR".
- quantity: the number only, without the unit (e.g. "24.5" for "24.5 L").
- One entry in line_items per product or service row, in the printed order. Do not include subtotal, tax or total rows as line items.
- If a value is not on the document or you cannot read it with confidence, use null. Never guess or calculate a missing value, except the tax sum described above.
